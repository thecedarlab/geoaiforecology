About

Sushil Paudel is a PhD researcher working at the intersection of geospatial AI, remote sensing, and forest ecology, and the founder of The CEDAR Lab.

Sushil Paudel

PhD Researcher · Founder, The CEDAR Lab

I’m a researcher working at the intersection of geospatial artificial intelligence and ecology. My work combines machine learning, remote sensing, and spatial analysis to study forest and landscape dynamics — understanding how ecosystems change and what we can do about it.

I founded The CEDAR Lab (Computational Ecology, Data Analytics & Remote Sensing) to advance open, reproducible research in geospatial ecology, and I run the GeoAI for Ecology YouTube channel to make these methods accessible to a wider audience.

Background

My research draws on forest ecology, landscape ecology, geospatial data science, and AI. I’m particularly interested in how we can use freely available satellite data and modern machine learning to answer ecological questions that matter for conservation and land management.

I believe in open science — the tools we build, the code we write, and the knowledge we generate should be accessible to everyone. Everything on this site, from the software to the tutorials, is released openly so that others can use, check, and build on it.
